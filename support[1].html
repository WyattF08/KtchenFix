Subscriptions

Purchases made through Apple are managed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions to view or cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase

Use the Restore Purchases option in KitchenFix while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Recipe or ingredient issues

AI-generated results can occasionally miss an ingredient or produce an unsuitable suggestion. Always verify ingredients, allergies, dietary restrictions, freshness, and safe cooking temperatures before preparing food.

Privacy requests

For account-data or deletion requests, email us with the subject line “KitchenFix Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your account before completing the request.

Response time

We aim to respond to support requests within 3–5 business days.
