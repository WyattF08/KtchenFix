Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Important: This policy is a practical launch template based on KitchenFix's described features. Review it before submission and update it whenever your app's actual data practices change.

KitchenFix (“KitchenFix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides tools that help users identify ingredients and generate recipe suggestions. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we may collect

  • Photos and ingredient information: Images submitted for ingredient recognition, manually entered ingredients, and information derived from those submissions.
  • Preferences: Dietary preferences, serving size, allergies, disliked foods, and other recipe settings selected in the app.
  • Account information: Information such as an email address or account identifier when account features are enabled.
  • Usage and diagnostics: App interactions, device information, crash information, and performance data used to maintain and improve the service.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status and transaction-related identifiers. Payment card details are handled by Apple and are not provided directly to us.

2. How we use information

  • To recognize ingredients and generate recipe suggestions.
  • To apply dietary and serving preferences.
  • To provide saved recipes, account features, and subscription access.
  • To maintain security, diagnose errors, and improve app performance.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Service providers

KitchenFix may use third-party service providers for cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, artificial-intelligence processing, authentication, and subscription management. These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices. Current or planned providers may include Apple, Google Firebase, OpenAI, RevenueCat, and Superwall.

4. Image and AI processing

Photos and text submitted to KitchenFix may be transmitted to service providers that help identify ingredients or generate recipes. Do not submit images containing sensitive personal information that is unrelated to the app's cooking functions.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and whether it is stored in a user account.

6. Your choices

You may stop collection by discontinuing use of the app. Where account features are available, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting us. You can manage or cancel Apple subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings.

7. Children's privacy

KitchenFix is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as KitchenFix changes. The revised effective date will be posted on this page.
